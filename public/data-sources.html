Australian Food Composition Database

Contains data from the Australian Food Composition Database – Release 3, © Food Standards Australia New Zealand (FSANZ). Retrieved 17 November 2026. Source database.

This derived dataset is supplied under the FSANZ Data User Licence Agreement, based on Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 3.0 Australia. You may reuse it under the same terms. FSANZ does not endorse this app.

Changes: selected nutrients mapped into the app’s schema; kJ divided by 4.184 for kcal; D2 and D3 summed when both are provided; missing cells omitted; specific gravity retained for volume conversions; metadata shortened. Vitamin A is retinol equivalents, folate is total folates. Vitamin K is unavailable in this source. Nutrient profiles can contain analysed, recipe, borrowed, imputed and estimated values; derivation is displayed. Foods and preparation differ from individual meals.

Data represents foods sampled at particular times and is not a guarantee of a current product label or individual intake. Download the derived dataset.

Open Food Facts

Contains information from Open Food Facts, made available under the Open Database License (ODbL) 1.0. Database contents are under the Database Contents License. The separate derived Australian-listed label snapshot and cached provider data retain those terms. No product images are redistributed.

Changes: selected reported nutriments mapped to 26 app fields, units normalised, label servings retained when their mass is known, no estimated-nutriment fields used. Community data must be checked against the current package. Presence in an Australian list does not guarantee current retail availability.

USDA FoodData Central

U.S. Department of Agriculture, Agricultural Research Service. FoodData Central. API guide and attribution. USDA data is public domain, published under CC0 1.0. Public DEMO_KEY searches have low limits. A private key can be used only through the optional server proxy.

Values use source units and nutrient IDs. IU values are not guessed into micrograms. Reported source conventions may differ from AFCD, particularly vitamin A and folate.

Quotes, stories & original artwork

The 100 quote excerpts are from Marcus Aurelius, Meditations, translated by George Long (1862), and Epictetus, Enchiridion, translated by George Long (1877). The translator died in 1879; these translations are public domain. Excerpt text is unchanged apart from whitespace. References and direct source links appear beside each quote. Marcus Aurelius source · Epictetus source.

Interpretations, questions and challenges are written for Strong Mind, not quotations from the philosophers. Seneca and Musonius Rufus are not included in this release: commonly accessible modern English translations have differing copyright terms. No unverified or falsely attributed social-media quotes are used.

Success-story summaries are original, concise accounts with direct source links and evidence labels in the app. Personal claims are distinguished from institutional records. No articles, interviews or third-party photographs are reproduced. Mountains and contour artwork were created for the app.
